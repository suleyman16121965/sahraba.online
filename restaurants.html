Bellapais Garden Restaurant

Bellapais Gardens Restaurant is one of the most highly regarded fine‑dining venues in North Cyprus, awarded the prestigious Certificate of Excellence by TripAdvisor. Set within a beautiful garden by the pool and overlooking the floodlit Bellapais Abbey, the restaurant offers an unforgettable dining atmosphere.

The menu features refined Mediterranean and Cypriot cuisine prepared with exceptional quality. Bellapais Gardens is known for its elegant service, premium wines, and peaceful ambiance, making it one of the top dining choices in Kyrenia.
