North Cyprus Travel Guide
Welcome to North Cyprus

North Cyprus is one of the Mediterranean’s hidden gems. With beautiful beaches, historic towns, friendly locals and affordable prices, it’s a perfect destination for holidays, long stays and exploration.

Main Regions

North Cyprus is divided into several key regions, each offering unique experiences:

  • Kyrenia (Girne): Harbour, castle, beaches, nightlife.
  • Famagusta (Mağusa): Palm Beach, ancient ruins, old town.
  • Iskele: Long beaches, resorts, calm atmosphere.
  • Karpaz: Golden Beach, nature, wild donkeys.
  • Lefkoşa (Nicosia): Capital city, museums, old streets.
  • Lefke & Güzelyurt: Nature, citrus gardens, peaceful villages.
Historical Sites

North Cyprus has thousands of years of history:

  • Kyrenia Castle
  • Bellapais Monastery
  • Salamis Ancient City
  • St. Barnabas Monastery
  • Othello Tower
  • Buffavento Castle
Best Beaches

North Cyprus is famous for its clean and natural beaches:

  • Golden Beach (Karpaz)
  • Alagadi Turtle Beach
  • Escape Beach
  • Glapsides Beach
  • Palm Beach
Transportation

- Main airport: Ercan Airport (ECN)
- Taxis available 24/7
- Car hire recommended for full freedom
- Buses available between major towns

Weather

North Cyprus has a warm Mediterranean climate:

  • Summer: 30–38°C
  • Spring: 20–28°C
  • Autumn: 22–30°C
  • Winter: 12–18°C
Culture & Lifestyle

North Cyprus is known for its hospitality, relaxed lifestyle, delicious food, and strong Mediterranean traditions.

Money & Visa

- Currency: Turkish Lira (TRY)
- Most tourists enter visa-free
- Credit cards widely accepted
