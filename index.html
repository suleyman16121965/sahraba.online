Discover Northern Cyprus

The Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus is the warm and welcoming side of the island, home to the ethnic Turkish Cypriot community. Like many Mediterranean peoples, Cypriots are known for their sunny nature, open hearts, and genuine hospitality. A simple walk through a village often turns into a delightful experience—children proudly greet visitors in English, smiling brightly as they ask, "Hello! What's your name?" Within minutes, you may find yourself embraced as a lifelong friend.

Every village revolves around its central square, usually marked by a statue of Mustafa Kemal Atatürk, the founder of modern Turkey and a beloved national figure. For local men, the coffee shop is the heart of social life. Here, strong Turkish coffee is enjoyed endlessly while lively backgammon games unfold. Life moves slowly, peacefully, and with purpose—a refreshing contrast to the fast pace of modern cities.

Village shops are charming treasure troves, selling almost everything imaginable. Some items may have sat on the shelves for decades—dusty packaging, faded labels, and old Turkish Lira price tags tell stories of a simpler time.

Beyond the villages, the countryside opens into carob orchards, citrus groves, olive trees, and fertile market gardens. The great Mesaoria Plain, known as the “Breadbasket of Cyprus,” transforms into a lush green sea of cereal crops each spring. By late May, the harvest is complete, and summer turns the plain into a warm, golden landscape where it’s hard to imagine anything ever grew.

The mountain roads of Northern Cyprus are safe, smooth, and perfect for scenic drives. Gentle breezes from the sea cool the air, stirring the pine trees and releasing their fresh, fragrant scent. Whether you're exploring ancient castles or simply enjoying the views, the mountains offer a peaceful escape into nature.
